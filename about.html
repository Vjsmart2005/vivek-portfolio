A bit about me

IDEAS INTO
VISUAL STORIES.

I’m Vivek Joshi, a video editor and visual storyteller based in Dehradun, India. For the past four years, I’ve helped turn ideas into films through considered editing, design, and motion.

I love the moment a collection of shots becomes something more: a rhythm, a mood, a point of view. From the first conversation to the final grade, I bring care to every frame and keep the story at the centre.

My work spans brand films, campaign edits, AI-led visuals, and social content. Whatever the format, the goal stays the same: make something honest, memorable, and worth watching.
